Yiyang Shao

I am a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at UC Berkeley, advised by Prof. Koushil Sreenath. Previously, I received my B.S. in Modern Mechanics from USTC.

I work on learning-based control, especially for humanoid robots. I am particularly interested in how world models and generative models can make reinforcement learning more scalable and general.
